01

Amit Kumar Singh Yadav

Amit Kumar Singh Yadav received his B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar, India, graduating with the Director’s Medal. During his undergraduate studies, he was awarded international scholarships including JASSO and Mitacs to conduct research in Japan and Canada, respectively. He went on to receive his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University, USA, where his doctoral research focused on self-supervised methods for speech forensics and on building interpretable, demographically unbiased forensic detectors for the DARPA SemaFor program. After his Ph.D., Amit worked as a Research Scientist at Meta, USA, where he worked on SpeechLLMs for speech recognition at Meta Superintelligence Lab and on post-training 400B-parameter multimodal LLMs at Meta Reality Labs. He is a co-founder of Fern (Ishiki Labs Inc.), a Y Combinator-backed startup. He has published more than 20 papers at international conferences, with research interests spanning media forensics, speech recognition, and multimodal LLMs.
